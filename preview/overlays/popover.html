Popover

A non-modal floating panel. Popover holds open state; PopoverTrigger clones its child button and wires aria-expanded; PopoverContent renders through a portal with computed positioning via computePosition. Configure with side (top | right | bottom | left) and align (start | center | end). Outside-click and Escape close. Focus returns to the trigger on close.

Bottom / center (default)

Positioned below the trigger, horizontally centered. Standard placement for command triggers.

Column widths

Set the width of each column in your view.

Right / start

Positioned to the right of the trigger, aligned to its top. Handy for row-inline settings pickers.
