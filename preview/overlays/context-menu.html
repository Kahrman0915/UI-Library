ContextMenu

Right-click / long-press activated menu. Same shape as DropdownMenu — with matching Item, Label, Separator, Group, CheckboxItem, RadioGroup, RadioItem, Shortcut exports — plus SubTrigger and SubContent for nested menus. Items support an error variant for destructive actions.
