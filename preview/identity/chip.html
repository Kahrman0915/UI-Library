Chip

A compact toggle-shaped button. Common uses: filter pills, tag selection, view toggles. Three sizes (xsmall, small, default) and a binary active state that flips the fill to --primary. Supports IconLeft, IconRight, and IconCenter (icon-only mode). Renders as a native <button> with aria-pressed.

States

Inactive is the default; active flips background to --primary and text to --primary-foreground.

Sizes

Three sizes; matches the density of surrounding content (compact filters, standard tags, larger tap targets).

With icons

IconLeft / IconRight for adornments, IconCenter + no label for icon-only.

Filter row

Typical usage — a horizontal group of chips for facet filtering. Only the active facets are highlighted.
