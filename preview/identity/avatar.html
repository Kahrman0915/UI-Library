Avatar

A person or resource identity marker. Renders src as an image, falls back to initials from fallback when the image is missing or fails to load. Two shapes (circle, square), three sizes (sm, default, lg), and a badge slot for status dots or counts. AvatarGroup stacks multiple avatars with configurable overlap and a +N overflow.

Sizes

Three sizes. Applies to both shapes; fallback typography scales.
