CloseButton

A compact X icon button for dismissing dialogs, closing panels, or removing items from a list. Two variants: default (transparent, follows text color) and background (translucent chip that reads against imagery or colored headers).

Variants

default is the everyday variant. background adds a subtle translucent chip so the button stays legible on top of a colored background.

Disabled

Applies the standard disabled state — 50% opacity, no pointer.

In context

Common placement — top-right of a dismissible panel or dialog. The background variant is useful over dark or colored headers.
