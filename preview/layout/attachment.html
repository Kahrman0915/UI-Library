Attachment

File / resource tile with lifecycle states. Composed of Attachment (root, holds state, size, orientation) plus slots: AttachmentMedia (icon or thumbnail), AttachmentContent (title + description), AttachmentActions (trailing buttons), and an optional AttachmentTrigger (invisible overlay linking the whole tile). Five states: idle, uploading, processing, error, done. The title shimmers during upload/processing.

States

Same content, different lifecycle stage. States are visible in the border, the shimmer, and the action set.
