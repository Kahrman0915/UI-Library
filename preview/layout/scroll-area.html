ScrollArea

A scrollable container with an unobtrusive, tokenized thumb. Custom-drawn (not browser-native) so it looks consistent across macOS / Windows / Linux and stays out of the way. Three orientations (vertical, horizontal, both) and two types (auto — thumb visible only when overflow; always — thumb always visible). Zero JS deps — the component measures its own viewport and positions the thumb on scroll.

Vertical

Fixed-height container with a long content list. The thumb sits inside the viewport, tokenized to --muted-foreground at low opacity.
