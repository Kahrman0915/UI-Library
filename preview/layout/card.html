Card

Neutral surface container for grouped content. Composed of Card + optional CardHeader (overline, title, description, action slot), CardBody, and CardFooter. The header is prop-driven (overline, title, description, media, action, showDivider) rather than slot-based, so common usages are a single JSX line. size lives on Card and cascades padding and type to every region (sm · default · lg · xl · 2xl) — the parts have to move together or their left edges misalign.

Header + body + footer

Canonical composition. The action slot in the header holds a button, badge, or icon-button; the footer is a bottom row that's typically right-aligned buttons.
